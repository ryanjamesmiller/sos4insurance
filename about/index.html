About Ryan

20+ Years Building Sales Systems That Actually Work

After 20+ years in high-performance sales and leadership, I've worked with owners and sales professionals in the insurance industry to build the kind of sales infrastructure that creates consistent revenue, accountable teams, and a process that doesn't fall apart when you're not in the room.

The insurance pros I work with are great at relationships. What they're missing is a repeatable process for turning those relationships into a predictable pipeline.

SOS — the Sales Operating System — fixes this. It's not a motivational program. It's a working system that diagnoses exactly where your sales process is breaking down and gives you a clear path to fix it.

I also believe deeply that a great business and a great life aren't two separate goals. After surviving the Route 91 shooting in 2017, I made a decision to stop separating who I am from what I do. My faith isn't a side note. It's the foundation. Every system I build, every conversation I have, and every leader I develop is grounded in that truth.

At the core, I'm a follower of Jesus, husband to Michelle, and father to Alexis and Chloe.

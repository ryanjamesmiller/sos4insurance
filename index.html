Frequently Asked Questions

What Agency Leaders Ask Before We Start

Clear answers to the questions that should not require another meeting.

How is this different from a one-day workshop or generic sales training?

A workshop teaches a skill for a day. We install a system — the Target Lock, the 1440 sequence, the Value Cadence System, and the Force Multiplier — inside your agency over 6 to 12 weeks, and we stay in the build until your producers are running it without you.

What if my producers resist doing things a new way?

Every engagement includes coaching support and weekly forum calls built specifically for adoption: role-play, real-time troubleshooting and accountability. Producers who see qualified meetings landing on their calendar tend to stop resisting fast.

How much time does this take from me and my team?

The heaviest commitment is the four live build sessions. After that, the weekly forum calls are for your producers, and the system runs through them, not through you.

How is payment structured?

A deposit to begin, with installments tied to build milestones. Solo advisor engagements can be paid in full or split into three payments.

Do you work with agencies outside group benefits and commercial lines?

SOS is built for both sides of the commercial house: group benefits and voluntary, and commercial P&C. Targets and triggers are built around how each side buys, including renewal dates and x-dates. It isn't built for personal-lines-only agencies.

We already have a sales process. Why would we need this?

Most agencies we work with do, and it usually works well once a producer is in the room. The Pipeline Power System builds the part most processes skip: who to pursue, how to open the door, and how to stay present until the prospect is ready to move. Your process is one room. SOS is the house. As one producer put it after the first session: "We don't have to completely rebuild it; we just have to refine it. That's a lot less overwhelming."

How do we keep producers from promising what our agency can't deliver?

Every Personal Runbook gets a red-flag review before it reaches a producer. We check every script for claims, promises and language your agency can't stand behind, and leadership gets a summary of anything we flagged. You see what your producers will send before a prospect does.

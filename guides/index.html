Straight Answers on Building an Agency Pipeline

By Ryan James Miller, founder of SOS

  • How Do Benefits Agencies Prospect for New Employer Groups?Benefits agencies that grow on purpose write down their target, qualify every employer group before the first touch, and run a 14-touch, multi-channel sequence timed to the renewal date.
  • How Do I Get My Producers to Prospect Without Me?Producers stop prospecting when the target lives in the owner's head. Give each one a written target, scripts in their own voice, a weekly number and a weekly room for live deals.
  • What Is a Good Prospecting Sequence for Commercial Insurance Producers?A good sequence is qualified, multi-channel, value-first and finite. The 1440 is 14 touches over 40 days across 6 channels, timed to renewal dates and x-dates.
  • What Should Go in an Insurance Producer's Ideal Client Profile?A useful ideal client profile names the size, industry, pain profile, fit signals and decision makers, plus the triggers that make now the time to reach out, and a pass/fail check before anyone gets pursued.
  • How Many Prospects Does a Producer Need to Hit Their New Business Goal?Work backward from the goal: wins needed, first appointments needed, touches a week, hours a week. In one real Runbook a $250,000 goal came out to 35 touches and 5 hours a week.
  • How Should Insurance Producers Follow Up When a Prospect Says "Not Right Now"?"Not right now" is usually the renewal calendar talking, not a no. Recap within 24 hours, then run a 7-touch, 21-day value sequence that ends with an earned ask to reconnect.
  • How Can Insurance Producers Use AI for Prospecting Without Sounding Like a Robot?Use AI to sharpen what a producer already wrote from real accounts, not to generate prospecting from nothing. Start with your own material, make it check your standards, and keep the relationship human.
  • How Do I Get a New Insurance Producer Prospecting Faster?New producers stall because they're handed a desk and a CRM login instead of a system. Give them a written target, scripts in their own voice, a weekly number and a weekly room from week one.
  • Is Sales Training Worth It for Insurance Agencies?A one-day workshop rarely changes production, because nothing is installed when it ends. What works is a system built inside the agency, materials each producer owns, and weeks of coached execution.
